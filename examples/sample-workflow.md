# Sample workflow

1. Capture: `ptm_capture` with content “我想学习香山处理器”
2. Agenda: `ptm_agenda` for today
3. Plan: `ptm_plan` create `time_block` or `time_budget`
4. Execute: `ptm_record_execution` with approximate minutes
5. Review: `ptm_review` for the past week
6. Reminder: `ptm_reminder` create; later `ptm_reminder_dispatch` claim/ack
